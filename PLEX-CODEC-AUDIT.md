## Search-string cheatsheet (avoid re-adding HEVC/DTS material)

**Look for / include:**
- `x264`, `h264`, or `AVC` — explicit video codec tag
- `1080p`, not `2160p`/`4K` — the Apple TV (4th gen) only outputs 1080p anyway, and
  4K releases are almost always HEVC
- `AAC` or `AC3`/`DD5.1` — audio codecs that Direct Play cleanly here

**Avoid / exclude:**
- `x265`, `HEVC`, `H265`, `H.265`
- `10bit` — almost always implies x265 even when the name doesn't spell out HEVC
- `DTS`, `DTS-HD`, `TrueHD`, `Atmos`
- `REMUX` — near-always paired with lossless/high-bitrate audio and, on newer
  titles, HEVC video too

**Group-specific gotcha:** `GalaxyRG265` in a release name literally encodes "x265"
in the group name — that's what caused all 8 Marvel titles above. Its sibling group
`GalaxyRG` (without "265") typically ships x264 versions of the same titles.

## Re-running the scan later

```sh
ssh ds214play 'sh -s' < scripts/scan-plex-codecs.sh
ssh ds214play "cat /volume1/PlexMediaServer/AppData/codec_scan_results.txt" > /tmp/codec_scan_results.txt
ssh ds214play "rm -f /volume1/PlexMediaServer/AppData/codec_scan_results.txt"
```

Worth re-running after adding new media, to catch HEVC/DTS additions before they
turn into a stuttering surprise on the Apple TV.
